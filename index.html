Our Mission – Midwest Weather

Midwest Weather was built on a simple principle: information should be accessible to everyone.

The Midwest Weather website and bot are completely free to use. We do not charge subscriptions, lock features behind paywalls, or restrict access to essential weather information. Our goal is to provide reliable updates, tools, and alerts to our community without barriers.

This project operates under Kurrasch Gaming Merch Store LLC as part of our broader commitment to community-driven technology. While some of our business ventures operate commercially, Midwest Weather is a public-focused initiative designed to serve — not sell.

We believe in transparency, accessibility, and consistency. Whether you're checking forecasts, using the bot, or browsing updates, you'll always have open access without hidden fees.

Customer and community satisfaction remain our top priority. The Kurrasch Gaming Merch Store LLC Customer Care Team is committed to maintaining responsive support, clear communication, and dependable service across all platforms we manage.

At Kurrasch Gaming Merch & Kurrasch Systems, we stand behind our projects — and most importantly, the communities that rely on them.

ℹ️ Important Information

  • Alerts are sourced from official weather services (NWS for the US, DWD for Germany; Canada, Australia and UK feeds are not connected yet)
  • Notifications go out as soon as the alert job processes an official issuance
  • You can manage subscriptions anytime with /alerts-manage or on this page
  • DM alerts require allowing DMs from server members
  • Do not rely solely on these alerts for life-safety decisions
Delivery to Discord, webhooks and email is performed by the Midwest Weather alert job reading these same subscriptions. Until that job is running against this database, subscriptions are saved and visible but not delivered.
